## Non-Negotiable Requirements

- All code must meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
- Never introduce a Critical or Serious accessibility regression.
- Persona cards must be usable with keyboard-only navigation and screen readers.
- All images must have meaningful `alt` text or be marked decorative (`alt=""`).
- Colour must never be the sole means of conveying information.
